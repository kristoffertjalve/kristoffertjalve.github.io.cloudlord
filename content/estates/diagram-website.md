[Diagram.website](https://diagram.website) is an internet map which also serves as my curatorial landscape. It features 200+ links to different internet philosophies and movements. The feeling I'm trying to provoke is that of  the starry night in a remote area because sometimes it is hard to see the depth and scale of the universe when caught inside an urban environment, or in the case of the internet, when we are stuck inside social media.





                    I believe handmade maps offer an intriguing alternative to navigate space. They are not trying to make a 1:1 representation of reality, but to recall walks that are eithter important or worthy - and to share them with others.
