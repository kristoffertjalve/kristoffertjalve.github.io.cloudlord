[Naiveweekly.com](https://www.naiveweekly.com/) is a living archive of the Poetic Web.

It documents the sites, books, people, and places that make up an emerging internet culture rooted in care and curiosity for the web. Through interviews, observations, and collected links, Naive Weekly traces the people and ideas shaping how we live with the internet.

The project began in 2018 as a Sunday email with links to the quiet, odd, and poetic web. Today, it has grown into an independent publication and an ever-expanding archive of internet culture.

Available on the web, through RSS, and as an infrequent newsletter.
