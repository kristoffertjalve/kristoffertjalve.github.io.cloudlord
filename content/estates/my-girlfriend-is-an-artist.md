[Mygirlfriendisanartist.com](https://Mygirlfriendisanartist.com) is a personal love project dedicated to my girlfriend, the artist and photographer [Ana Šantl](http://iamnotana.com/). 

Before I met Ana, I noticed her photographs. They bring a soft tenderness to any subject, a calm invitation to perceive the world differently. Her work is the visual aspiration for all of my work with the internet, and how I practice to move through life.

The website presents a collection of Ana's horizontal analogue photographs, many of which would otherwise remain unpublished. Clicking reveals another photograph, accompanied by a short text written by me. The site was made as a birthday gift and is preferably viewed on something larger than a phone.
