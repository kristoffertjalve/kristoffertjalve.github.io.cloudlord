[Mygirlfriendisanartist.com](https://Mygirlfriendisanartist.com) is a personal love project. On every refresh, the website features a different of her horizontal photographs combined with a short text written by me. The site was made as a birthday gift and prioritises our shared memories.





                    My girlfriend, [Ana Šantl](http://iamnotana.com/)'s analogue photographs are the visual aspiration for all of my work with the internet. Her photographs bring a soft tenderness to any subject, a calm invitation to perceive the world differently.
